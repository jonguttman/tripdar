--- PROMPT ---

Studio product photograph of {product_name} by {brand}, a {format} cannabis/mushroom
product. Place the exact product, unchanged, on a seamless pure-white (#FFFFFF) studio
background. Soft, even, large-softbox key light from upper-left, subtle fill, gentle
natural grounding contact shadow directly beneath the product. Product perfectly
centered, upright, occupying ~76% of frame height, square 1:1 composition, generous
even margins. Crisp commercial e-commerce catalog look, high dynamic range, true-to-life
color, sharp focus edge to edge, no reflections, no props, no text overlays, no added
graphics.

HARD CONSTRAINTS (label fidelity):
- Do NOT alter, repaint, translate, re-typeset, hallucinate, or "clean up" ANY text,
  logo, barcode, dosage figure, warning, or artwork on the product label.
- Do NOT change product shape, proportions, packaging, cap, or fill level.
- The label region must be preserved pixel-faithful from the source; if the generator
  cannot guarantee this, ABORT and route to human review — never ship an altered label.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
extra products, duplicated product, warped label, garbled text, misspelled text,
invented ingredients, invented dosage, added logos, watermark, hands, people, colored
background, gradient background, harsh shadows, blown highlights on label, plastic look.

--- END PROMPT ---
